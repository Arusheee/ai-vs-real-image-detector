Method

Upload, classify, verdict

01

Upload

The image you choose is sent to the classifier running on the server for analysis.

02

Model inference

A ResNet18 classifier, fine-tuned on real and AI-generated images, scores the image and outputs a probability for each class.

03

Verdict & confidence

The probability is compared against a calibrated threshold to produce a final verdict and confidence score.
